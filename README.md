## 📖 About the Project

An interactive Power BI dashboard for the Food & Beverage industry that studies 10,000 consumer survey responses across 10 Indian cities. It helps CodeX, an energy drink brand, understand who its consumers are, what they prefer, where and why they buy, how competitors are seen, how they discover the category and how CodeX itself is performing.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- 👥 Understand who the respondents are by age, gender, city and tier
- 🥤 Understand what consumers expect from an energy drink
- 🛒 Understand where, when and why consumers purchase
- 🏁 Analyse the competitive landscape and brand perception
- 📣 Understand how consumers discover the category and why some never try it
- ⭐ Evaluate CodeX awareness, trial, taste experience and perception

## 🗃️ Dataset

Consumer survey data with 10,000 respondents across 10 cities (Ahmedabad, Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Jaipur, Kolkata, Lucknow, Mumbai and Pune), including demographics, preferences, purchase behaviour, current brands, marketing channels and CodeX feedback. 980 respondents are CodeX consumers.

## 📒 Dashboard Pages

- 🏠 **Home** – landing page with navigation to every report
- 👥 **Demographics** – who the respondents are
- 🥤 **Consumer Preference** – what consumers expect from the product
- 🛒 **Purchase Behaviour** – where, when and why consumers buy
- 🏁 **Competition Analysis** – existing brands and brand perception
- 📣 **Marketing** – channels, awareness and reasons for not trying
- ⭐ **CodeX Performance** – how CodeX is performing among respondents

## 📋 Reports

1. 👥 **Demographics** – respondents by age, gender and city, market channels by age, and consumption reasons by age.
2. 🥤 **Consumer Preference** – ingredients expected, packaging preferences by age, interest in organic or natural products, health concerns and price segmentation by gender.
3. 🛒 **Purchase Behaviour** – typical consumption situations by age, consumption frequency by gender, purchase locations, price range by city and consumption reasons.
4. 🏁 **Competition Analysis** – current brands, reasons for choosing a brand, general perception of each brand, city-wise respondents versus CodeX and overall brand perception.
5. 📣 **Marketing** – CodeX heard and tried respondents, never-tried respondents by city, reasons for not trying, marketing channels, consume time by age and limited-edition packaging.
6. ⭐ **CodeX Performance** – city-wise CodeX count, desired improvements, taste experience, price range, general perception by gender and CodeX heard count.

Every page can be filtered by **Age**, **Tier**, **Gender** and **City**.

## 💡 Key Insights

- 👥 The 19–30 age group is the largest segment with 5,520 of the 10,000 respondents
- 👨 Males make up about 6.0K respondents, females 3.5K and non-binary 0.5K
- 📍 Bangalore (2,828), Hyderabad (1,833) and Mumbai (1,510) have the most respondents
- 🥇 Cola-Coka (2.5K) and Bepsi (2.1K) lead the current brands, while CodeX has about 1.0K
- 🏷️ Brand reputation (2.7K) is the top reason consumers choose a brand, followed by taste (2.0K)
- 📣 Online ads (4.0K) are the biggest marketing channel, followed by TV commercials (2.7K)
- 🚫 The top reasons for not trying CodeX are not available locally (2.4K) and health concerns (2.3K)
- 🛍️ Supermarkets (4.5K) are the most common purchase location, followed by online retailers (2.6K)
- 🏃 Sports and exercise is the most common consumption situation, especially for ages 19–30
- 💰 The 50–99 price range is the most popular (4,288), followed by 100–150 (3,142)
- ☕ Caffeine is the most expected ingredient (3.9K), and 5.0K respondents are interested in organic or natural options
- 📦 Compact and portable cans are the most preferred packaging (3,984)
- 🌱 Reduced sugar content is the top improvement consumers want (3.0K)
- ⭐ CodeX has 980 respondents, 488 who tried it and an average taste rating of 3.3
- 📍 CodeX is strongest in Bangalore (292), Hyderabad (182) and Mumbai (156), and weakest in Lucknow (5)
